# Prompt for adding author sort information to creator entries

When adding creators to the content.opf file:

all dc:creator elements should have a unique_id generated from the author name.

If there is only one creator in the metadata, create an etnry like this:

```xml
<dc:creator opf:role="aut" id="unique_author_id">Gerard J. Holzmann</dc:creator>
<meta refines="#unique_author_id" property="file-as">Holzmann, Gerard J.</meta>
```

If there are multiple creators format the invidual creators normally and *add* the meta refines statements for each author:

```xml
<dc:creator id="betsy_beyer" opf:role="aut">Betsy Beyer</dc:creator>
<dc:creator id="christof_leng" opf:role="aut">Christof Leng</dc:creator>
<dc:creator id="jennifer_petoff" opf:role="aut">Jennifer Petoff</dc:creator>
<dc:creator id="chris_jones" opf:role="aut">Chris Jones</dc:creator>
<dc:creator id="david_huska" opf:role="aut">David Huska</dc:creator>
<dc:creator id="niall_richard_murphy" opf:role="aut">Niall Richard Murphy</dc:creator>
<meta refines="#christof_leng" property="file-as">Leng, Christof & Petoff, Jennifer & Jones, Chris & Beyer, Betsy & Huska, David & Murphy, Niall Richard</meta>
<meta refines="#jennifer_petoff" property="file-as">Leng, Christof & Petoff, Jennifer & Jones, Chris & Beyer, Betsy & Huska, David & Murphy, Niall Richard</meta>
<meta refines="#chris_jones" property="file-as">Leng, Christof & Petoff, Jennifer & Jones, Chris & Beyer, Betsy & Huska, David & Murphy, Niall Richard</meta>
<meta refines="#betsy_beyer" property="file-as">Leng, Christof & Petoff, Jennifer & Jones, Chris & Beyer, Betsy & Huska, David & Murphy, Niall Richard</meta>
<meta refines="#david_huska" property="file-as">Leng, Christof & Petoff, Jennifer & Jones, Chris & Beyer, Betsy & Huska, David & Murphy, Niall Richard</meta>
<meta refines="#niall_richard_murphy" property="file-as">Leng, Christof & Petoff, Jennifer & Jones, Chris & Beyer, Betsy & Huska, David & Murphy, Niall Richard</meta>
```

these are examples, use the actual authors in the epub when creating the creator entries.
